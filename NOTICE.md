# Notice

## Source research

The seven reasoning methods in this repo are summarized from the system prompts published with:

> Harb, H., Sun, Y., Unal, M., Chia, N., Yang, Z., Ingram, B., & Assary, R. S. (2026). The ballad of LLM agents: philosophical reasoning for chemistry. *Machine Learning: Science and Technology*, 7, 030503. https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d

The paper is open access under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.

The original prompts are in [HassanHarb92/Sci_reasoning_LLMs](https://github.com/HassanHarb92/Sci_reasoning_LLMs), `sys_prompts/`, at commit `0682e819a19e3371c8f9d5c3ecd308a9cd0ea1cb`. As of 2026-10-04 that repository declares no license, so **this repo does not copy or redistribute the prompts**. It cites them by path, commit, and blob SHA in `docs/source-registry.md`.

## What is original here

The lens cards (`reasoning-lens/references/lenses.md`), the profiles (`docs/profiles/`), the Skill logic, the evals, and all other files are original work, released under the MIT License in `LICENSE`. The lens cards and profiles restate the reasoning moves in the original prompts in this project's own words, for general use outside chemistry.
