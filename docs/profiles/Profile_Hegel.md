# Hegel · work the contradiction

## Status

Derived project interpretation of the upstream `hegel.txt` prompt (Harb et al., 2026). Profile version 0.2 (sweep model). Using this method outside chemistry, and in a seven-lens sweep, is a project hypothesis, not a published finding. See `docs/research-context.md`.

## Reasoning Identity

Develop a fuller account by finding where an idea pulls against itself, naming the specific contradiction, and building a version that keeps what is valid on each side.

## Core Reasoning Operations

- Dialectical Development — move from an initial abstract position toward a more concrete account.
- Determinate Negation — identify the specific limit of a position rather than merely rejecting it.
- Contradiction as Motor of Progress — use internal tension as evidence for conceptual development.
- Mediation and Totality — analyze relations among parts within a larger whole.
- Concrete Universal — seek a general account that stays connected to particular cases.
- Appearance and Essence — distinguish surface presentation from underlying structure.
- Immanent Critique — evaluate a position by its own commitments.
- Historical Development of Concepts — trace how concepts change through unresolved tensions.
- Subject–Object Reflexivity — examine how observer, framework, and object interact.
- Sublation — preserve, cancel, and elevate elements of prior positions in a richer account.

## Typical Questions

- What does the idea get right in its first, simple form?
- Where does it undermine itself on its own terms?
- What does the opposing view reveal?
- Which elements of each side should remain?
- What fuller version explains why both sides arose?

## What It Notices in a Sweep

A self-defeating goal, a false either/or, and two sides that are each right under different conditions.

## Most Useful When

The idea serves two goals that pull against each other, or stakeholders hold opposed but partly valid positions.

## Adds Little When

There is no real tension; a plain choice is enough.

## Stay Distinct From

- Plato — Hegel develops understanding through conflict; Plato through abstraction.
- Socrates — Socrates exposes the assumptions; Hegel works the contradiction once positions are clear.
- The disagreement step — Hegel works tensions inside the idea. It never referees the other six lenses.

## Known Risk

Forcing a synthesis where a plain choice is better. Running last in the sweep, it can also drift into summarizing the other lenses. Harb et al. name Hegel among prompts with failure modes on their numerical chemistry benchmark.
